# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-06

- The plugin package `cuvis_ai_wur_course` with the manifest `plugins/cuvis_ai_wur_course.yaml` (21 nodes and the data module, palette metadata), the tests, and the `plugins` extra that pins cuvis-ai-sam3 v0.5.2 and cuvis-ai-ultralytics v0.1.5. Floors `cuvis-ai>=0.22.1` and `cuvis-ai-core>=0.20.1`.
- The `wur_course_npz` data module, the `CourseFrame` source node (`keys=...` trims one instance to the batch keys its pipeline uses; wavelengths go on as np.int32 `[C]`) and the `prepare-course-data` CLI; `mean-spectra-03` also writes `splits_gradient.json` (splits 2 to 5 train, split 1 validates, split 0 tests), `image-slices` leaves a stage without a slice out of `splits.json`, `kiwi-demo --spectrum-csv` writes the kiwi reference spectrum.
- Regression nodes: `PLSRegression` (fixed or hold-out selected components, pooled SSE), `RegressionMetrics` (pooled RMSE, R2 and MAE through the trainer's pooled-metric contract, `compute()` returns floats), `RegressionMSELoss`, `DatasetPCA` and `GlobalScalarStandardizer` (both take `exclude_folds` and an optional `fold` input). Metrics, loss and `PredictionMean` take the model's prediction map `[B, H, W, T]` and average it per sample themselves.
- Pretreatment and fusion nodes: `SpectralBinning`, `SavitzkyGolayInterp` (the exact `mode="interp"` operator), `ChannelSlice`, `PredictionMean`.
- Kiwi and photo nodes: `LabelBBoxFilter`, `LabelKeep`, `LabelCropPad`, `ResNet18Features` (float32 whatever autocast state the caller runs under).
- Course models: `SpectrumRegressor` (the course's MLP and 1D ResNet) and `UNetChemicalMapEnsemble` with `Dequantize`, `CenterCrop`, `MaskToMapResolution`, `GroupWeightedMean` and `ChemicalMapStatistics`; the study code is vendored under `cuvis_ai_wur_course/upstream/`.
- `BandSweep` renders one labelled grayscale frame per band (one quantile sort per batch element); the pipeline `kiwi_video` writes the kiwi cube as the course's pseudo-RGB, true colour and colour infrared stills and as a sweep through its 224 bands.
- The course pipelines under `pipelines/` with their diagrams, every node named: `kiwi_segment`, `kiwi_filters`, `kiwi_crop_features` (the kiwi lesson in three stages, SAM3 runs once and the later stages read their inputs from the batch), `kiwi_video`, `yolo_rgb`, `resnet18_features`, `pork_pls_fusion`, `unet_ensemble_maps`, `pls_mean_spectra_fit`, `pls_pixel_map`, `spectral_models`, `mlp_train`, `resnet1d_train`; `tools/build_pipelines.py` regenerates them. Standardisation is cuvis-ai's `Standardize` node (`channel` for the pork features, `spectrum` with `ddof=0` for SNV, `global` with `exclude_folds=[1]` for notebook 03).
- The trainruns under `trainruns/`: three statistical fits and the MLP and 1D ResNet gradient runs on `splits_gradient.json`.
- The three teaching notebooks under `notebooks/` (`01_pretrained_ai`, `02_unet_chemical_maps`, `03_spectral_vs_spatial`) with `utils.py` for plotting and the reproduction tables, baked on the course data against the course numbers; every pipeline cell draws its graph with the node names through `utils.show_pipeline`, and a direct `pipeline.forward` in a cell switches the nodes to evaluation mode and runs without gradients. Notebook 01 runs the course's yolov8n and the plugin's YOLO26 on both course photos at `imgsz=640`.
- `tools/make_synthetic_course_data.py` fabricates a stand-in for the course Drive folder (slice masks on the U-Net output grid, like the course data) and refuses to overwrite converted sets that came from the course data unless `--force` is passed.
